About TechFest

TechFest 2026 is an exciting technology event organized by ABC College. The event provides students with an
opportunity to demonstrate their technical knowledge, creativity and problem-solving skills.

Students from different courses can participate in coding competitions, web development challenges, quizzes,
project exhibitions and other exciting activities. The event is designed to encourage innovation, teamwork and
learning.
